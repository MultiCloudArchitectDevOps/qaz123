Select Column

The Select Column functionality allows users to add/remove column(s) as required. The Select Column functionality displays how many columns are selected out of the total available columns.

To add/remove the column(s), follow the steps below:

  1. To access the Digital Content Manager page, follow steps 1 to 5 of the  Access the Digital Content Manager process under the Authoring Hub section of the user guide.
  1. Click the Select Column icon.

Select Column icon

  1. The TOGGLE COLUMNS drop-down opens.

TOGGLE COLUMNS drop-down

  1. To hide any Column(s) the user wants, click the checkbox(s).

Column(s) selection

  1. The checkbox(s) gets deselected.

Deselected checkbox(s)

  1. The deselected Column(s) gets removed.

Updated Column(s) list

  1. To add any Column(s), Click the Select Column icon.

Select Column icon

  1. The TOGGLE COLUMNS drop-down opens.

TOGGLE COLUMNS drop-down

  1. Click the empty checkbox(s).

Column(s) selection

  1. The checkbox(s) gets selected with a checkmark icon.

Column(s) checkbox(s) selection

  1. The selected Column(s) gets added.

Newly added Column(s)
