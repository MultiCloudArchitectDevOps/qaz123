The Logs tab displays information about actions performed on each Document, including changes made at specific dates and times.

Access the Logs

To access the Logs page and check all/total Logs, follow the steps below:

  1. Navigate to Grid Menu button located on the upper left side of the window. Click the Grid Menu button.

Grid Menu button

  1. The Grid Menu gets expanded.

Expanded Grid Menu

  1. Click the Production Hub expand button.

Production Hub expand button

  1. Click the Collaborate button.

Collaborate button

  1. The Collaborate window opens.

Collaborate window

  1. Click the Logs tab.

Logs tab

  1. The Logs window opens.

Logs window

  1. Users can see/check Total Logs here.

Total Logs
